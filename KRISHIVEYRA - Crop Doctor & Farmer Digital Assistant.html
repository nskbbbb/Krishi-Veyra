Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

Scientific Name: Solanum lycopersicum

Moderate Risk
Urgent Action

Prune off severely diseased bottom leaves today with clean shears, then spray Mancozeb 75% WP (2 spoons in 15L pump) tomorrow morning.

Diagnosis Summary

Tomato Early Blight is causing fungal ring-shaped brown lesions with yellow edges on lower foliage. If untreated, it causes defoliation and fruit rot.

Visible Symptoms & Damage

Concentric target-board rings on older, lower leaves
Yellow chlorotic halos surrounding brown spots
Premature drying and dropping of bottom leaves
Dark sunken spots near the tomato fruit stem

Safe Chemical Solution

Exact Spoons & Pump Dose
Mancozeb 75% WPBrands: Dithane M-45, Indofil M-45, Uthane
Spoons per 15L Backpack Pump:🥄 2 to 3 level tablespoons (30-35g) per 15-liter backpack sprayer tank
Best Spray Time:Early Morning (6:30 AM - 8:30 AM) when leaf surface is dry
Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SCBrands: Amistar Top, Custodia
Spoons per 15L Backpack Pump:🥄 1 cap / 1 tablespoon (15 ml) per 15L pump sprayer
Best Spray Time:Late afternoon (4:30 PM - 6:00 PM)

Organic & Home Remedy Option

🌿 Sour Buttermilk & Copper Wire Extract

Take 1 liter of sour cow buttermilk (churned curd), immerse a clean copper wire/plate in it for 5 days in a plastic container until it turns greenish. Dilute with 10 liters of clean water.
